A little bit about me

I'm an experienced digital product and UX/UI designer based in London. My work has spanned institutional fintech and investment platforms, insurance and banking, internal tools for large organisations, and smaller product-led teams. That includes compliance and admin workflows on ZILO Invest, Zurich's Client Portal, and mobile flows for super, health, and everyday banking.

Over time I've built a strong eye for detail and a practical human-centred process: understanding how people actually work, and shaping interfaces that are clear under pressure and give users confidence in what they're doing.

I aim to create designs that work for users day to day and hold up for key stakeholders. For me that means research and workshops, close collaboration with engineering and product teams, and patterns that extend beyond a single feature into a coherent design system.

I'm particularly interested in how we design for AI, and how AI is changing the design process. If you'd like to know more about my experience or discuss a role or project, get in touch via email or LinkedIn below.
